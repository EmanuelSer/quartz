## Chapter 1 | Daniel and the dawn The Human Destiny.

The story of an intern joining a space research group in Mexico. He's nervous and afraid but excited  to be working on something trascendental for humanity.

One day he goes home to find his girlfriend has disappeared. She being the only family he has.

He follows some clues here and there and tries to juggle his new job with his search for his girlfriend.

Things are getting wild on the streets. A war seems to be on the horizon.

The research group finds some interesting data coming from one of the potential planets they sent probes to.

He finds his girlfriend was taken by a military group because she was involved with the war somehow.

The team finds that there is intelligent life elsewhere in the galaxy but they're on their infancy.

She is murdered. War erupts.

The team is going to escape. They too are targets for the opposing side because they're intellectuals and thus dangerous to the new regime.

They give him the data from the probe asking him to keep it secret.

He flees to another country and publishes an article revealing the location of a solar system rich in some mineral needed by humans and suggests that there is NO signs of intelligent life on the galaxy and we should stop looking because that's a waste of resources. We are alone.

His text becomes incredibly trascendental but without his girlfriend he doesn't really care for anything anymore. Disappointed of humanity he retreats somewhere over the mountains.

Daniel had been away for only a few days, but as he walked into his apartment, he could feel that everything had changed. The silence that greeted him was deafening and only exacerbated by the distant sirens of the planes flying above. The emptiness was palpable, as if the very air inside the apartment had been sucked out. He could sense her absence in every corner, every room, every inch of the space they had shared. He knew, with a sinking feeling in his gut, that she was gone.

He desperately searched for any sign she might had left, but all he found were her empty shoes and her watch still ticking away on the nightstand.

He looked at the file on his desk and remembered the day he was called in to work at AEM. He had been so excited. The letter had arrived in the morning bearing the seal of the space agency. It was a summons, and to Daniel it meant a new life, a new destiny.

## Chapter 2 | The writer and his obsession with the dream world.

A writer who seeks for inspiration looking at the stars. One day he comes across a patch in the sky that seems rather empty but he notices that it flickers in a recognizable pattern. The pattern turns out to be a website hosting a VR game where you experience from afar the slow but steady evolution of humanity. After this experience the writer becomes obsessed with this micro world as he feels like he is a God to them. He develops a strong bond for some of the small humans and he goes out of his way to try to protect some of them in the game. After a few weeks he begins dreaming about this world and that's how he comes up with the stories in his books

David Rothman had always looked to the stars, searching for the light of inspiration, despite knowing there was nobody out there. He usually found comfort in their presence, like they were distant friends. However, on this particular night he found nothing but darkness.

## Chapter 3 | The three proposals for human expansion and the violent death of the most promising mind.

In the future, humans have predicted the planet will die within 100 years due to a cosmic catastrophe. In response the governments of the world have united to develop a series of plans that will ensure the survival of the human race. After a few years they narrow it down to 3 plans that they will execute in the span of the last century, in hopes that at least one of them will succeed.

The clock was ticking, and the fate of humanity hung in the balance as the leaders of the world gathered for the first conference of the Survival Initiative. They had been given one century to save humanity from certain doom, and today, they would reveal the projects that would determine the fate of the human race. It was a desperate gamble, with three possible paths to salvation. The room was filled with tension. It was a momentous day, one that would decide the future of humanity and their legacy.

The details of each proposal were discussed with extreme caution and deliberation, their feasibility analyzed, and the resources allocated. But there was one voice that was not heard in the room, the voice of a young detective.

## Chapter 4 | The detective investigating the murder.

Right after the presentation of the last proposal at the Survival Initiative, that of Dr. H.S. Where he revealed his controversial plan to send a light vessel containing only a human DNA virus with the objective of finding a host on a habitable planet and forcing it to eventually evolve into what we now know as humans, the vessel would be immediately followed by a large ship at a speed of .01 percent the speed of light, containing tons of human knowledge and technology. Hoping that, by the time the second ship arrives, humans will be sufficiently evolved to be able to decipher and understand the contents of the ship. Dr. Seldon was also about to reveal they have found evidence of intelligent life elsewhere on the galaxy, but just as he was about to say the words, he was murdered.

There had been suspicion of a rebellious organization attempting to sabotage the Survival Initiative project for a long time but they never thought life’s were in danger, much less those of the academics leading the research to save our species. Because of that suspicion detective Kira Novak was placed within the audience when the murder took place. She was a very clever woman. She desperately worked to uncover the facts surrounding Dr. H.S.’s murder, but the investigation was made harder by the political and economic implications the whole affair was causing.

"There's one more thing," Dr. H.S. had said, right before he died. The words still hung to Kira’s mind as she combed through the evidence with single-minded determination. It was already past midnight, she was now alone in her office but she kept reliving the moment over and over in her mind’s eye.

A black jacket, brown running shoes, and a peculiar string that hung from her waist. Who was the killer? Kira felt like the answer was just within her reach, yet still, she could not understand the pieces of the puzzle.
