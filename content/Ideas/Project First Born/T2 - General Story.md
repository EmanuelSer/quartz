# T2 - General Story

The story of an intern joining a space research group in Mexico. He's nervous and afraid but excited  to be working on something trascendental for humanity.
One day he goes home to find his girlfriend has disappeared. She being the only family he has.
He follows some clues here and there and tries to joggle his new job with his search for his girlfriend.
Things are getting wild on the streets. A war seems to be on the horizon.
The research group finds some interesting data coming from one of the potential planets they sent probes to.
He finds his girlfriend was taken by a military group because she was involved with the war somehow.
The team finds that there is intelligent life elsewhere in the galaxy but they're on their infancy.
She is murdered. War erupts.
The team is going to escape. They too are targets for the opposing side because they're intellectuals and thus dangerous to the new regime.
They give him the data from the probe asking him to keep it secret.
He flees to another country and publishes an article revealing the location of a solar system rich in some mineral needed by humans and suggests that there is NO signs of intelligent life on the galaxy and we should stop looking because that's a waste of resources. We are alone.
His text becomes incredibly trascendental but without his girlfriend he doesn't really care for anything anymore. Disappointed of humanity he retreats somewhere over the mountains.

A detective finds some unusual activity going around the moon city.
Software engineers are disappearing left and right.
The events coincide with the disappearance of a probe sent to a distant solar system to find minable planets.
A new religion is gaining traction. The detective suspects they are behind the unusual activity.
He finds that the religion is a suicide cult of people who want to transcend this reality.
The detective confronts the head of the religion who happens to be the author of the famous We are alone text.
They reveal their purpose of creating life (G A.I.) and kill themselves on the spot.
Millions of people across several planets commit suicide.
The detective is sad.

The greatest assassin in the galaxy  is foolishly spotted during a mission on planet earth. She tried to locate the witness but fails.
She thinks of herself as an agent of entropy and only kills those she deems contributors of evil to the universe. She wants to maximize the amount of happiness in it. For this she happens to have an special ability.
A writer had struggled with getting published for the longest time until one day he daydreams a fantastic story. He doesn't know where the story came from but he manages to publish it in a well respected magazine. It becomes rather popular and they aske him to write some more.
The witness contacts the assassin and reveals he wants to hire her services to stop a thief.
The writer duels on the fact that he doesn't know how to get the inspiration again. He is attacked by a gang one day and a man in a suit suggests him to stop writing that kind of stories because it doesn't match the views of the Martian society. \[Connect with the revolution maybe?\]
The assassin follows and investigates her target who turns out to be the writer. Wondering why the witness want him dead or why he thinks he fits her criteria she decides to postpone the kill.
She turns to investigate the witness instead. The assassin discovers the true identity of the witness: the detective.
The writer delivers a new story for the magazine, this time written in a different style. His usual uninspired style. They turn down his proposal asking him to serialize his previous stories instead. He then tries to activate his daydream again.
This is how the assassin and the writer cross paths for the first time. She can't kill him because he is contributing greatly to the universe's entropy.

They confront the ai. They take their own decisions. Humanity is ready.
