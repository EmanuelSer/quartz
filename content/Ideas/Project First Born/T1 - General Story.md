# T1 - General Story

This is the story of a scientist who was working along with a few others to search within the stars for a place to gather resources from.
They loved working on this project because of the incredibly rewarding sense of accomplishment that came with something like this. To feel like you were contributing to a higher cause, to humanity at large.
It all worked out fine for the first few months. Their research was promising and they had found several potential systems to look further into but this stage was short-lived. Because China cut the water supply of their country and the rumor of  a war coming became more and more common. Because their research was government funded, their budget shrank. The team became smaller and smaller and they had to limit the scope of their ambitions.
They became laser focused on finding systems similar to our own in their chemical composition whereas before they had even talked about searching for intelligent life.

Our main character was particularly bumped down by this limitation of their scope because they longed for external intervention in the rising tension between powers and dreamed of finding some intelligent beings who would talk us out of nuclear war. This, however didn't happen.
World war 3 erupted just as they received data from solar system 002. There they found some patterns that suggested life that thriving there.
But our main character was scared for them, for what would be of them if we humans destroyed even our own and they were clearly behind us in their development as a society.

When prompted to release their findings in order to decide whether or not continuing to fund the research, our character wrote an amazing blend between literature and research paper where they detailed the richness of elements in several solar systems and the definitive proof that we are alone in the universe.
This paper became famous for it and it's principles became the bases for several ideologies after the war.

So life was saved and left alone to continue developing while humans destroyed themselves and their planet.

Several years into the future, humanity managed to rise again. Civilization was able to pick up pretty much from where it left.
Borders changed as did a lot of what made human society. The new super powers were the North American Union, Chinese something and the New Turkish empire. With their expansion came a huge leap in technology that ultimately took us to Mars, Venus, Europa and the asteroids. The names of this nations didn't even made much sense anymore since their territory expanded outside the boundaries of earth.
But as much of civilization as it had changed some things remained the same and some others re-emerged from oblivion. Such as poverty, nationalism and injustice.

Our main character is a pilot. The very best there is. Destined to pilot the biggest ship ever created through the fastest highway in history to the farthest corner of the universe humanity had ever reached.
The mission was almost ready, the only thing missing was the actual ship which was under construction in Mars, as well as the data from a promising solar system a probe was sent to collect. But the political climate on the planet wasn't stable. So when the probe disappeared before reaching the system, a group of revolutionary Martians took hold of the planet and it's defense system in a swift coup-d'état. They believed the NAU had intercepted the probe to prevent them from finding an earth-like planet and moving there thus leaving the solar system without it's main labor and scientific force. On the other hand the NAU claimed it was Mars who was responsible of the disappearance, on the basis of them not wanting to share the resources found on that system.
It was on this chaos that our main character was abducted by the Martian revolutionaries. Who tried to convince them of helping their cause.

Years earlier, something similar had happen.
A probe was sent to collect data from this solar system and it disappeared before reaching it's destination. Although at the moment not much was though of it. The engineers in charged assumed a mistake on their behalf but switched their attention to the successful probes since they had sent one to every potentially mine-able solar system.
Only a handful of people where intrigued by the occurrence. Among them the CEO of the space company in charged of the rockets taking the probes to their destinations, he also happened to be one of the scientists who were working on the original project before the war. He hired a detective to help him investigate the matter.
This is how they came to learn of the existence of a religious organization that claimed the purpose of  humanity was to create life. To give birth to a new kind of consciousness that was better than us. The detective discovered they were mainly interested in converting computer scientist and because of that concluded that their objective was to create a General AI. They didn’t know, however, the reason behind their sabotage of the probe.
When the detective finally cracked down on the goal of the organization after completing the GAI it was too late. That day millions of people committed suicide claiming they were ascending to a higher state of existence. The GAI was left alone in the world. Nobody dared to speak to them, afraid they might brainwash them into killing themselves. Except for one person: the detective. Who wanted to know why they had tampered with the probe. When they found out about the truth they too were convinced that it was best kept a secret. Though for different reasons.

In the future, the pilot finds the GAI and confronts them about the irrationality of their actions. Humans are no longer destructive. We are ready for the truth.

(three probes were sent in total. The first by the scientists who first discovered life and hid the data. The second was intercepted and destroyed by the religious organization. The third was the one that ignited the revolution and was also intercepted but by the General AI who was tasked to keep the place a secret.)
