<p class="transition">FADE IN:</p>

### EXT. GRASS FIELD - DAY

MITTENS jumps out of a wooden drawer into the vast plain.

After a slow walk, he begins running as the sun casts golden rays on his path.

### INT. SARAH’S HOUSE - NIGHT

SARAH arrives home, ears buzzing, stomach growling. She devours the first thing she sees in the fridge.

A plate of cat food still half full sits in the dim lit corner.

An uncomfortable silence fills the room, she calls for Mittens.

She looks under the bed, in between the cushions, inside the closet, but after a futile search, she heads out with a pile of papers.

<p class="character">SARAH</p>
<p class="dialogue">Mittens!</p>

### EXT. CITY PARK - NIGHT

SARAH walks in the park, calling for Mittens while she puts up flyers.

### EXT. CITY ALLEY - NIGHT

SARAH calls out for Mittens, looking under parked cars and in the bushes.

### EXT. NEIGHBORHOOD STREET - NIGHT

SARAH continues her search in the neighborhood, calling for Mittens in dark alleys. No response. She eventually surrenders to defeat.

### INT. SARAH’S HOUSE - NIGHT

SARAH stares at an old photo of Mittens before going to bed.

### INT. TRAIN - DAY

SARAH, on a packed train, writes in her notebook. She sees through the corner of her eye Mittens out the window, then the train plunges into a tunnel and the wagon empties.

<p class="transition">CUT TO:</p>

### EXT. UNKNOWN TRAIN STATION - DAY

When it re-emerges, the landscape has changed to a countryside.

SARAH disembarks onto a deserted station. The place is strangely deserted, like a forgotten station in the middle of the mountain. She attempts to explore, but the sound of an approaching train takes her back.

### INT. TRAIN - NIGHT

SARAH wakes up still in her sit. The wagon is full once again. She disembarks and begins to wander the lonely streets of the familiar neighborhood.

<p class="character">SARAH</p>
<p class="dialogue">Mittens!</p>

### EXT. CITY PARK - NIGHT

In front of her apartment, SARAH cries on a park bench. Trains running in the distance. She opens her notebook, and begins to write.

### EXT. UNKNOWN TRAIN STATION - NIGHT

SARAH is back at the deserted station. She ventures into the vast darkness around the station, lured by a small beacon of light.

A distance "meow" calls her attention and she heads in the direction of the sound.

### EXT. GRASS FIELD - NIGHT

SARAH finds MITTENS near a glowing door. Startled, he jumps back into the drawer, she beckons Mittens to come to her. He slowly pokes his head out and as soon as he sees her jumps out of the drawer to meet her.

He rubs his body around her legs and begins purring, then as if remembering, walks excitedly towards the glowing door.

Sarah calls Mittens and motions towards the train station and into her bag. She pulls out a can of cat food and offers it to Mittens. Mittens inspects the food, then nuzzles her hand, and licks her face but walks towards the doors once more.

Sarah gets up, and smiles to Mittens through her tears. He changes his demeanor and jumps playfully towards the door where the silhouette of another cat starts to make visible. He looks back and slow blinks towards her.

Sarah stands in the middle of the grass plain field illuminated solely by the stars.

### INT. SARAH’S BEDROOM - NIGHT

SARAH walks into her bedroom, dimly lit by the glowing lights coming from the open windows, kneels before her dresser, retrieves a drawer from her dresser and stares at it.

Over her bed is an old photo of Mittens as a kitten inside the dresser’s drawer.

On the corner of the room, a half-eaten can of cat food.

Sarah cries her eyes out. A wry smile draws on her face. She stands up carrying the drawer.

<p class="character">SARAH</p>
<p class="dialogue">Goodbye, Mittens.</p>

<p class="transition">FADE OUT:</p>
