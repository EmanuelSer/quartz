In the middle of a gameplay a player realizes the NPCs have their own storylines and decides to follow one. What comes next is a documentary of their virtual life.

* * *

For further development: What kind of game is it? Is it a traditional RPG with a vast open world and many characters to interact with, or is it a more linear game with a set storyline and characters?

What kind of NPC does the player choose to follow? Is it a central character to the game's story, or a side character with a more subtle storyline?

How does the player's decision to follow this NPC affect the gameplay and their overall experience of the game?

What challenges does the player face in following the NPC's storyline? Are there other characters or events that conflict with their objective?

How does the player's virtual journey through the NPC's story affect them in the real world? Do they learn something about themselves or their own life through the experience?
