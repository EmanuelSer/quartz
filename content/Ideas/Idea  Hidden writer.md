title: Idea - Hidden writer
date: 2023-01-01
---


He had never intended for things to end up like this. But after a few nights staying at school in that hidden closet he found peace in the silence of the building. The way he found peace was through writing his thoughts into a book. He wrote for weeks and years before he finished his opus. Although nobody would ever read it. To read his book they would have to burn the plants that rose over the balcony hiding the secret room, a public fire was required.
