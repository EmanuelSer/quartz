The first thing I've read by **Naomi Kritzer** and she does not disappoint. This is such an **immersive story** that connects with the reader with interesting storytelling and a **deeply emotional plot**.

I've come to appreciate the work that is put on short stories, like this one in order to make them appealing.

Telling stories is not easy. You need to be entertaining and precise in your words. You need to tell the story but also create an **atmosphere** around it, construct the world that the audience is supposed to believe, captivate and surprise with an unexpected twist or satisfy with a **good ending**.

All of this is specially hard to do in **short formats** (as I will discuss in more detail when writting about "love, death, robots").

This short story was particularly relevant for me, not only because it does it's job wonderfully, but also because in it the author describes a lot of this **story-telling elements and challenges**.

I too love to hear ghost stories whenever the situation allows for it despite the fact that I do not believe in them. But I want to much the same way the main character does.

I was able to connect personally with the story because of that and that was perhaps the goal of **Naomi Kritzer**.

Her story doesn't have a plot twist but it really doesn't need one either. The **slow build up** works perfectly with such a **touching finale**.

A few months after reading the story I've come to a new conclusion.

I realize that the story feels so real and intimate thanks to the fact that Naomi Kritzer used some of **her real life story to fill the universe**. This is fairly normal practice in the storytelling universe but I want to make a distinction because I believe this short story is a **perfect exemple**.

There is a difference between those who tell their story and those who use bits of it scrambled in their fiction. This is an example of the later while a film like **"The third wife"** seems to be the former.

The main difference lies in the storyteller's ability to tell new stories that still retain the **magic and real intimate feeling**.
