The **directorial debut** of renowned cinematographer **Rodrigo Prieto**.

It might be that I am too easily impressed but this 8 minute short really stands out to me as **above average filmating**.

Goes without saying that the **lighting and camera work is impecable** save for a weird panning in an impossible movement. The work put on this short clearly shows the intent of it. That is, the lighting, the compositions, and the art direction, along with the make up designs, **complement each other to convey a message clearly**. Despite the fact that this message is not a simple one.

This of, course, speaks of the good work of the director as well as of the talented artists behind.

Truth is, the **story is shallow and a tad superficial** but the method used to communicate this idea is so meticulous I am impressed.

Wish I could come up with a short like this that would express so much about my directorial and cinematographic skills.
