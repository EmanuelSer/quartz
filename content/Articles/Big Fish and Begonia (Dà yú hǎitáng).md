This is a very absorbing narrative with an incredible artwork and likable characters. Immersing its audience in an enticing narrative complemented by spectacular artwork, this film offers a captivating viewing experience. To truly appreciate all of the accomplishments this film achieves, it is essential to address the film's strengths, while briefly acknowledging its weaknesses.

What I found the most polishes aspect it's definitely the world building. The film presents a complex world filled with magic and amazing scenery. The mechanics of the magic are not entirely explained, but we do see a lot of the inner workings of this world.
We know there are different kind of powers, we learn about the faith of human souls for those with a pure heart. It constantly shows new aspects of their world and one jut keeps wondering if it might not actually exist.

Secondly, the character design and character development it's a gem of it's own. Had the characters not be as interesting to watch as they were  I probably wouldn't have seen the film at all. From the color schemes to the way they transform to finishes and back again it's so eye catching and entertaining. 
Which leads me to my other point, the 2D animation is so beautiful. You see this magical scenes with lights and movement everywhere. However, the integration of 3D animation occasionally falters, as the divergent styles can disrupt the immersion of the narrative.

The story seemed to me incredibly original and imaginative. This may be due to my detachment from the Chinese culture and traditions. However I must say I haven't seen something as creative as this world in a long time. This masterful work of art stands as a testament to the limitless potential of the human imagination and the magic of storytelling.
