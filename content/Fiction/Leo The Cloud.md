# Illustrated Book Idea

Leo was only five years old when he decided he no longer wanted to be a human, instead he wanted to become a cloud to explore the world.

His parents were obviously worried but soon decided that it was just a phase and he would eventually grow out of it. They even helped him fulfill his dream by building him a cloud costume.

He would wear that costume and run around the neighborhood pretending to be a cloud and exploring as much as he could.

But Leo wanted to explore even farther! He wanted to see the mountains and the rivers of the world. If only he weren't bound to the earth and could just float away.

That night he went to bed with one wish in mind, and as he fell asleep he began his transformation. First he climbed to the top of the highest mountain in the world and spread out his arms. Then he breathed in deeply, and as he exhaled, his body began to change. His skin turned white and fluffy, and his limbs turned into wispy tendrils. Soon he was a cloud!

He floated on the breeze, higher and higher, until he was among the stars. He explored the solar system and beyond, learning all there was to know about the universe.

Eventually he tired of his travels and decided to return home. But when he tried to change back into a human, he found that he couldn't. He didn’t want to be a cloud forever!

But just then his mom walked into the room and woke him up. It was just a dream!

And from that day on he never wished to be a cloud anymore. He was content being a human and living with his family, but he would always remember his time as a cloud and all the adventures he had.
