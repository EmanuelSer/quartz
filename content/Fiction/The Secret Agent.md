After saving the lives of two people in a secret operation we walked the streets of the city in seemingly random patterns. Knotting out paths, trying to confuse whoever was following us, if there even was someone.

Once we felt confident in our loneliness we decided to head towards out bus stop.
We sat on a bench next to some scholar, under a great bridge three or four stories high, flanked by residential buildings one one side and commercial buildings on the other. The danger was now certainly gone and as the buzzing sounds of monotony started to become the center of attention, I had a feeling of familiarity suddenly wash over me. 
I knew this bridge. I remember I had taken the bus heading on the opposite direction at the top of the bridge once, going home after a long day at my university. Even though me, I had never attended a university. I had seated myself next to a girl I hardly knew but the commute became my ally and by the end of it I'm sure we had become close friends.
And there was this other time I was driving a sports car through the second level of the highway next to the apartments, hoping to get the attention of someone I knew lived on them.

I couldn't remember much after that for our bus was finally coming and my body was starting to wake up. But the realization had settled on me.
This was the city of dreams, a place I'd visited many times over, where every person holds a story, an adventure, a secret, a dream; where I've been many people and never myself.
