I had a dream about someone I was reluctant to meet. Apparently, we had some history and for some reason, the people around me were trying to make a reunion happen without my knowledge.

I don’t remember much about her, only that she was Chinese. However much I tried, the reason why we parted ways still eluded my memory. I saw her again crossing through the door, coming into the house. I had a vague feeling that she was better off without me.

Afraid of confronting her and her mom, and conflicted about letting my friends know about the mess my heart was after my relationship with her, I thought of fleeing. Anything but being vulnerable again. Not now, not here, with her. 
Through the window at the door I saw her. As our eyes met, I found her as beautiful as ever. But in those eyes I saw something missing. The lie she used to tell me had vanished. She didn't love me, she couldn't.

She smiled, and an unbearable feeling of sadness took hold of me.

I realized I could do anything to stop and escape the situation. In a panic, I thought of dissociating but when I tried it, I woke up.

As I laid on my bed, tucked in under a light sheet with the slight hum of a fan barely noticeable, I felt the cold of the all encompassing darkness and all I could think of was her. The memories already fading away, I started to question my decision of escaping. But it was too late.

All I had had, I had given up. The friends, the achievements, the love I had had, all because of fear. Was the confrontation really all that bad?

None of it mattered anymore. I was someone else now, my brain was filled with memories of another, New people now surrounded me, and I had a different name.
