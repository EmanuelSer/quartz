I was walking alone around some city with way too much time on my hands. The city felt big but I kept circling the same places, afraid of venturing too far and being late to an appointment I had.
I walked into a morning diner that looked like it had seen better days. I sat near a window reflecting on my options when I realized I didn't really want to be there. 
On the opposite side of the street there was a coffee shop I had a feeling I had wanted to visit for quite some time, always finding excuses not to. The truth is the place looked expensive and the people there very different from me.

I don't recall what made me finally walk in there. Perhaps there too I thought of her. She'd become an image of a girl with expensive taste whom I would never be able to understand. I don't know how much of that was real. I could no longer remember her as vividly as I once did, and she hardly ever appeared in my dreams anymore. Nothing but an image.

Regardless, I remember ordering their homemade croissant muffin, likely the cheapest thing on their menu. Then I felt silly about ordering just bread and threw in a large coffee too. The sum was large. Not larger than expected but still a lot for a half-ass breakfast.
I handed them my card while reassuring myself I had enough money for the transaction. It was going to go through and they would never know they were taking half my funds for the week. I would blend in and I promised myself I'd forget about the money once I sat down. 
Their terminal, however, had a different plan as it shut itself down in the middle of the transaction. They apologized and I offered to come back after I had finished my coffee and they had had time to re-charge their terminal. But when the opportunity arose I caught myself thinking of running away. 
I was ashamed, knowing, truly, I would never be able to blend in.
