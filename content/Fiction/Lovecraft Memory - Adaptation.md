## FADE IN:

### EXT. VALLEY OF NIS - NIGHT

The moon shines over the valley of Nis, creating a path of light through the foliage of a great upas-tree. Vines and plants move slowly, creeping amidst the stones of ruined palaces, twining around broken columns and strange monoliths, and lifting up marble pavements.
On gigantic trees little apes leap, while serpents and scaly things without a name writhe over treasure-vaults.

### INT. DEPTHS OF THE VALLEY - NIGHT

Where the light hardly reaches, move unrecognizable shadows. There are moss covered giant stones scattered around the floor, and tall stone walls from where they might have fallen. At the very bottom of the valley lies the river Than, whose waters are slimy, red, and filled with weeds.

<div class="character">GENIE</div>
<div class="dialogue-description">(O.S.)</div>
<div class="dialogue">I am old, and forget much. Tell me the deeds and aspect and name of them who built these things of stone.</div>

The DEAMON OF THE VALLEY is sitting in the shadows, eyes glowing red. Looks at the moon and responds.

<div class="character">DAEMON OF THE VALLEY</div>
<div class="dialogue">I am Memory, and am wise in lore of the past, but I too am old. These beings were like the waters of the river Than, not to be understood. Their deeds I recall not, for they were but of the moment. Their aspect I recall dimly, for it was like to that of the little apes in the trees. Their name I recall clearly, for it rhymed with that of the river. These beings of yesterday were called Man.</div>

The Genie flies back to the thin horned moon. The light recedes and the valley is left in shadows.
The Daemon of the Valley looks intently at a little ape in a tree that grows in a crumbling courtyard.
