There was once a man of modest means who wished for nothing but wealth, as one does when brought up in the harshness of scarcity, and by way of fate's twisted design, one day he made a deal with a mysterious old man, who would provide him with strange contraptions and tools never before seem, for him to sell. The old man brought an array of all sorts of trinkets and the man didn't need but to advertise them to the townsfolk.
However, after a time, a proposition was presented to him, urging him to unearth the secret and find where the old man got his strange supply from, to cut the middle man, and thus enhance his own gains.

Lead by greed, the man followed the enigmatic figure along the shadows to an abandoned building at nightfall, where the old man crept to the button of the building. Lowering below it's very foundations. With a key, he cleared the passage through a dormant portal hidden amidst the darkest corner. Anticipation clicked in the man's heart as he entered the chamber and was presented with an immense collection of artifacts. And overwhelmed by ambition, he attacked the old man and stole the key. His weak skull offered little resistance and the man thought his plan flawless when suddenly, deep within the bowels of the strange chamber, he heard a voice call his name. Drawn towards the source, he found himself venturing deeper, into a cave excavated within the building's primitive stone walls. There he saw tools and artifacts stranger still, gadgets of the most bizarre nature, a hand uncannily human, detached from a body, capable of following his mind's commands. 
So fear gripped him with an iron fist, driving him to flee the place. He sealed the mysterious doorway behind him, discarding the stolen key and seeking refuge in the mundane comfort of his home.

But alas! even at home he never found peace again. Nothing ever offered him rest from his dread. For he could still hear the eerie voice, echoing from the depths of the strange room, murmuring his name. And that sound haunted him in every wakeful moment, robbing him of his tranquility and leaving him in perpetual terror.
