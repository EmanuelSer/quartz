### EXT. TOKYO PARK - NIGHT

YURIKO (Black formal dress) and NATSUME (White on black polka dots and jeans) are sitting on a park bench in an empty Tokyo park. The city's buzz is audible but distant. Yuriko, with her legs crossed and her hands on her knees, stares at her fingers. Natsume, curious, watches her.

<div class="character">YURIKO</div>

<div class="dialogue">You are standing at the entrance to a forest, shortly before dawn. All around you still reigns the most absolute darkness, you don't quite know why you are in a place like this. Nevertheless, you walk straight ahead and enter the forest.</div>

### EXT. FOREST - NIGHT

As the protagonist, a person whose face we never see, walks deeper into the forest they spot a small house.

<div class="character">YURIKO</div>
<div class="dialogue-description">(V.O.)</div>

<div class="dialogue">A little further on, you see a small house. You open the door carefully. Inside are ten sleeping children.</div>

### INT. SMALL HOUSE - NIGHT

The protagonist observes the ten sleeping children. They seem to be in a deep slumber, oblivious to their surroundings.

She notices their bellies going up and down.

<div class="character">YURIKO</div>
<div class="dialogue-description">(V.O.)</div>

<div class="dialogue">The children are fast asleep. There is neither joy nor happiness, nor, of course, sadness or pain. There is nothing. Because they are all sleeping. You can choose whether you are going to wake them all up or let them sleep.</div>

### EXT. TOKYO PARK - NIGHT

Yuriko's face now deeply serious, absorbed into the world she is describing.

<div class="character">YURIKO</div>

<div class="dialogue">If you wake them all up, there will be nine of the ten children who will be happy. They will thank you heartily for waking them up. But the last one will not. You know that for this child, from the very moment of birth until death, only suffering worse than death itself awaits. You know that he will live to death in pain. You do not know what it is. But you know that one of the ten children will not be able to escape it.</div>

Yuriko stops narrating and looks at Natsume, who is deep in thought.
